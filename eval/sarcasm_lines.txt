# Tricky lines for eyeballing the interpreter. One utterance per line.
# Mix of sarcasm, passive aggression, polite refusals, and plain literal
# sentences — the literal ones are here on purpose to check the model isn't
# over-reading tone into neutral speech.

# --- sarcasm ---
Oh great, another meeting that could have been an email.
No, please, take your time. It's not like I have anywhere to be.
Wow, you actually showed up on time today. Miracle.
Sure, because that plan worked out so well last time.
Nice job breaking the build again, really impressive stuff.

# --- passive aggression ---
Fine. Whatever you think is best.
It's fine, I'll just do it myself like always.
Must be nice to have time for lunch breaks.
I already told you twice, but sure, I'll explain it again.
Thanks for finally getting back to me.

# --- polite refusals ---
That sounds lovely, but I don't think I can make it.
I appreciate the offer, I'll have to pass this time.
Let me check my calendar and get back to you.
I'd love to help, but my plate is really full right now.
Maybe some other time — thanks for thinking of me though.

# --- plain literal ---
The meeting is at three in conference room B.
Can you send me the report by end of day?
I had a sandwich for lunch.
The train was ten minutes late this morning.
Thanks, that actually really helped me out.
