emtext hears a microphone, not a person. Without a voiceprint it cannot tell your lines from anyone else's, so it interprets your own words and explains your feelings back to you. Once enrolled, your lines are still transcribed (they are what the other person is replying to) but never read.

Read these aloud

Say each line the way the note suggests, and pause about a second between lines — that silence is how the server splits them. The moods matter: a profile built only from calm speech stops recognising you exactly when you are upset, which is when this app is needed.

  1. I'm going to make some tea, do you want one?
    calm, ordinary
  2. Oh that's brilliant, I didn't think it would work.
    genuinely pleased
  3. I've told you three times already, it's on the table.
    annoyed, a bit sharp
  4. Yeah. No, I'm fine. Just a long day.
    tired, quiet, flat
  5. Wait, seriously? When did that happen?
    surprised
  6. Yeah, sure.   No, not really.   Okay.
    three quick short replies, a pause between each
  7. You're unbelievable, you know that?
    warm, laughing
  8. Great. Just perfect.
    deadpan, no energy at all
