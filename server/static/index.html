Press Start, allow the microphone, and talk. Each sentence gets a transcript, a colour-coded read of what it likely means, and — when speech emotion recognition is on — how the voice itself sounded.

Try the same line two ways to see the voice change the read:
“Oh wonderful, that's just perfect.” — once brightly, once flatly.
“You are unbelievable, you know that?” — once warmly, once angrily.

Pause for about a second between sentences: that silence is how the app knows a sentence ended. A read takes a few seconds to arrive.
